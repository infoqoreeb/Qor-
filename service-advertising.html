Performance Advertising

Paid advertising is the fastest way to put your book in front of readers who are actively looking for their next favorite read. But most authors waste significant portions of their ad budgets on poorly targeted campaigns, weak creative, and mismanaged bidding strategies. Our Performance Advertising service eliminates that waste and replaces it with data-driven precision.

Our Approach

Multi-Platform Ad Management Built for Books

We manage advertising campaigns across all major book marketing platforms: Amazon Ads (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Lockscreen Ads), Facebook and Instagram Ads (through Meta Ads Manager), BookBub Ads, and Google Ads when appropriate for your genre and goals. Each platform serves a different purpose in your marketing funnel, and we deploy them strategically rather than spreading your budget thin across every option.

Amazon Ads are the foundation of most book advertising campaigns because they reach readers at the exact moment they are browsing for books to buy. We build comprehensive Amazon Ads campaigns that target competitor titles, genre-specific keywords, category-based audiences, and product attribute targeting. We continuously monitor search term reports, adjust bids based on actual conversion data, and expand into new keyword territories as we identify profitable opportunities.

Facebook and Instagram Ads allow us to reach readers based on their interests, behaviors, and demographics rather than their search intent. This makes them ideal for building awareness, driving email list signups, and retargeting readers who have already shown interest in your book. We develop custom audiences, lookalike audiences, and retargeting funnels that move readers from initial awareness to purchase over time.

BookBub Ads are particularly effective for reaching voracious readers who actively follow deals and recommendations on the platform. We design BookBub Ad campaigns that complement your other advertising efforts and target the specific subgenres where your book is most likely to resonate.

What We Handle

Complete Campaign Lifecycle Management

Audience Research and Targeting

We analyze your genre's competitive landscape to identify the specific reader profiles most likely to purchase your book. This includes analyzing competitor audiences, identifying high-value keywords, mapping reader interest clusters, and building detailed targeting parameters for each platform.

Creative Development and Testing

Ad creative is the single biggest variable in advertising performance. We develop multiple ad variations for every campaign, testing different headlines, images, copy angles, and calls to action. We use A/B testing frameworks to identify the highest-performing combinations and scale them aggressively.

Bid Optimization and Budget Allocation

We manage your daily and monthly budgets across all platforms, shifting spend toward the campaigns and keywords that generate the best return on investment. We monitor cost-per-click, click-through rate, conversion rate, and cost-per-sale to ensure every dollar is working efficiently.

Reporting and Strategic Adjustments

You receive regular performance reports that break down every metric in clear, understandable terms. We do not just hand you a spreadsheet of numbers; we explain what the data means, what adjustments we are making, and what results to expect going forward. All reports and strategic discussions are delivered via email at info.qoreb@gmail.com.
