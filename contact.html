Let's Talk About Your Book

The best way to reach us is through email. Send us a message and we will get back to you with a thoughtful, detailed response within 24 to 48 hours.

Why Email Only?

At Qore'eb, we conduct all of our communication exclusively through Gmail. This is a deliberate choice, and there are several important reasons behind it.

First, email creates a permanent written record of every conversation. When we discuss your marketing strategy, your campaign performance, your budget allocation, or your launch timeline, every detail is documented and searchable. You never have to rely on memory or worry about miscommunication.

Second, email allows us to provide more thorough and thoughtful responses. Complex marketing strategies cannot be adequately discussed in a rushed conversation. When we respond to your questions via email, we take the time to provide complete, detailed answers that you can reference repeatedly.

Third, email respects your time and ours. You can read and respond to our messages on your own schedule, whether that is early in the morning, late at night, or during a break in your writing routine. There is no need to coordinate availability or block out time for appointments.

Response Time

We respond to all inquiries within 24 to 48 business hours. If your inquiry is urgent, please indicate that in your subject line and we will prioritize accordingly.

What to Include

When you reach out, it helps if you include the following information: your book's genre and subgenre, your publication status (published, upcoming, or in progress), your primary marketing goals, and any specific challenges you are currently facing. This allows us to provide a more relevant and useful initial response.
