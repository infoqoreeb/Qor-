About Qore'eb

The Strategist Behind Your Book's Success

We are not a generic marketing agency that applies the same template to every author. Qore'eb is a specialized book marketing consultancy built on the understanding that every manuscript carries a unique audience, a unique market position, and a unique set of challenges that demand a tailored strategic response.

Our Story

Built From the Ground Up for Authors

Qore'eb was founded with a singular mission: to close the gap between what authors deserve and what the publishing industry typically delivers when it comes to marketing support. The reality is that most publishers, including many of the largest houses in the world, allocate minimal marketing budgets to the majority of their titles. Authors are frequently left to figure out book promotion on their own, navigating a landscape of conflicting advice, expensive courses, and service providers who overpromise and underdeliver.

We built Qore'eb to be the antidote to that experience. Every strategy we develop, every campaign we execute, and every system we build is designed around one question: will this measurably move the needle for this specific author and this specific book? If the answer is not a confident yes, we do not deploy it.

Our name, Qore'eb, reflects our deep connection to the world of books and reading. We understand the emotional investment that goes into writing a manuscript. We understand the vulnerability of putting your work into the world. And we understand the frustration of watching a book you poured years of your life into disappear into the marketplace without the visibility it deserves. That understanding is the foundation of everything we do.

Our Philosophy

Strategy First, Execution Always

Data Over Guesswork

Every decision we make is informed by real market data, competitive analysis, and audience research. We do not rely on trends, hunches, or what worked for someone else three years ago. We analyze the current landscape for your specific genre, your specific subcategory, and your specific target reader profile before recommending a single tactic.

Infrastructure Over Tactics

Most book marketers focus on individual tactics: a Facebook ad here, a newsletter swap there, a blog tour somewhere else. We focus on building complete marketing infrastructure. That means email systems that nurture readers over months, advertising funnels that compound over time, and brand platforms that generate organic visibility long after a campaign ends.

Transparency Over Hype

We will never promise you a bestseller list placement, a viral moment, or overnight success. Those outcomes are possible, but they are the result of sustained strategic effort, not guarantees anyone can make honestly. What we do promise is complete transparency about what we are doing, why we are doing it, what results to expect, and what the realistic timeline looks like.

Long-Term Over Short-Term

A successful book launch is important, but it is just the beginning. We build marketing systems designed to sell your book for years, not just during launch week. Our strategies account for the long tail of book sales, the compounding effect of reader reviews, and the cumulative power of a growing author platform.

How We Work

A Process Built on Precision and Communication

When you begin working with Qore'eb, the first thing you will notice is that we ask a lot of questions. Before we write a single line of ad copy, design a single graphic, or launch a single campaign, we need to understand your book inside and out. We need to understand your goals, your timeline, your budget, your existing platform, your competitive landscape, and your ideal reader profile.

All of our communication happens through Gmail at info.qoreb@gmail.com. We operate exclusively through email for a very specific reason: it ensures that every conversation, every decision, every revision, and every strategic recommendation is documented and accessible. You will never have to wonder what was discussed or what was agreed upon because the complete record is always available in your inbox. This system keeps both parties organized and accountable, and it allows us to reference previous conversations when making future strategic decisions.

Our typical engagement follows a structured process. First, we conduct a comprehensive audit of your current marketing position. Second, we develop a customized strategy document that outlines every tactic, timeline, and expected outcome. Third, we build the necessary infrastructure, which may include email sequences, landing pages, ad accounts, social media content calendars, and more. Fourth, we execute the campaigns with ongoing optimization based on real performance data. Fifth, we provide detailed reporting so you always know exactly what is happening and why.

Who We Serve

Authors Across Every Genre and Career Stage

Qore'eb works with fiction and nonfiction authors across every genre. Whether you write epic fantasy, literary fiction, romance, thriller, science fiction, horror, memoir, self-help, business, health and wellness, Christian living, young adult, middle grade, poetry, or any other category, we have the genre-specific knowledge and strategic frameworks to position your book effectively in its market.

We work with first-time authors who are preparing to launch their debut novel and have no existing platform. We work with midlist authors who have published several books but have never had the marketing support they need to break through to the next level. We work with established authors who want to optimize their existing systems and scale their results. We work with traditionally published authors whose publishers are not providing adequate marketing support. We work with self-published authors who are building their careers independently and need professional-grade marketing infrastructure.

The common thread among all of our clients is a commitment to treating their writing career as a serious professional endeavor. We are not the right fit for authors looking for a quick fix or a magic button. We are the right fit for authors who understand that book marketing is a strategic discipline that requires expertise, patience, and sustained effort.

Why Qore'eb

What Sets Us Apart

Book-Specific Expertise

We do not market restaurants, software companies, or e-commerce stores. We market books. That singular focus means we understand the nuances of book discovery, the mechanics of Amazon's algorithm, the psychology of reader purchasing behavior, the seasonal patterns of book sales, and the specific platforms where readers congregate and make buying decisions.

Genre Fluency

Marketing a cozy mystery requires a fundamentally different approach than marketing a business leadership book or a dark romance novel. We understand the conventions, reader expectations, competitive dynamics, and promotional channels that are specific to each genre. This fluency allows us to craft campaigns that resonate authentically with your target audience.

Full-Stack Capability

Most book marketing providers specialize in one area: ads, or social media, or email, or publicity. Qore'eb provides the complete spectrum of book marketing services under one roof. This means your campaigns are integrated and cohesive rather than fragmented and disconnected. Your email sequences support your ad campaigns. Your social media content reinforces your brand positioning. Your launch strategy connects to your long-term growth plan.

Documented Processes

By operating exclusively through Gmail, we maintain a complete documented record of every interaction. This is not just about convenience; it is about accountability and precision. When we reference a strategic decision made three months ago, we can pull up the exact conversation. When you want to review the rationale behind a campaign adjustment, the explanation is right there in your inbox.
